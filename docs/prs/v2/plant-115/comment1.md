The record is generated by plant-dev's `harness/long_drought.R`. Wet
days follow a seasonal two-state Markov chain and a wet day's depth is
gamma-distributed; each year's depths are scaled by a multiplier drawn
between 0.85 and 1.30, years 8-10, 19-22 and 32-34 by 0.28 to 0.55, and
four years by 1.5 to 1.65. It is then rescaled to a mean of 3.0 m/yr.
9.5% of its 14 965 days are wet. The model reads it through a cubic
interpolant with a knot at each day; 2931 of those knots fall inside the
40-year stand where the rain is not zero on both sides. The stand is one
species at lma 0.32, 108 introductions spaced evenly over 40 years, on
the birth-date coordinate, at `ode_tol` 1e-4.

## Terms

- **knot**: a time where the rain's interpolant changes its cubic, here
  each day of the record. Between two knots the rain is smooth.
- **substep**: a Cash-Karp step of the soil alone, inside one step of the
  stand, between two of its own ends. Its ends land on every stage time
  of the stand's step and on every knot inside it.
- **uptake fraction**: at the start of a step, the plants' uptake as a
  fraction of all the water moving through the soil,
  Σ|uptake| / Σ(|inflow| + |drainage| + |uptake|), summed over the five
  layers (`TF24_Environment::uptake_fraction_of_flows`). Below
  `Control$ode_soil_substep_max_uptake` (default 0) the plants barely
  move the soil, and the step substeps the soil whether or not it spans a
  knot.
- **input slope**: the trend of each layer's uptake since the last
  adaptive step, along which the uptake is extrapolated across a
  substepped step.

## Control flow

```
  odelia adaptive step from t, size h                    (odelia 0.8.1)
    │  Patch::subsystem_knots(t, t+h) non-empty,
    │  or Patch::subsystem_weakly_coupled()?
    │
    ├─ no ─► the step as before: six stages of the whole state
    │
    └─ yes ─► choose_substeps: the soil alone (Patch::subsystem_rates,
              the five layers' rates under the uptake extrapolated along
              the input slope), Cash-Karp, error-controlled, its ends on
              every stage fraction and every knot
              │
              ├─► the six stages: the plants from the tableau; the soil
              │     read from its substeps at each stage time
              ├─► the soil again over its ends, under the uptake on the
              │     line to the uptake at the stage at t+h (corrector)
              └─► error estimate: the tableau's for the plants, the
                    coupling's for the soil; the step's record keeps
                    {input_slope, ends}
  replay, invasion, reverse sweep: take each recorded step's
  {input_slope, ends} as recorded
```

## What changes for existing code

| old | new |
|---|---|
| a variable-rain run without stops reads the rain at the six stages only | substeps the soil across each knot; {{MOVES}} |
| a run with a zero-depth `rainfall_pulse()` at every knot | unchanged, bit for bit (the stops leave no step spanning a knot; `ode_soil_substep_max_uptake` is 0) |
| `sched$set_ode_steps(times, sizes)` | `sched$set_ode_steps(times, sizes, input_slopes, substep_ends)` |
| `p$ode_times`, `p$ode_step_sizes` | joined by `p$ode_soil_input_slopes`, `p$ode_soil_substep_ends` (empty by default); a run reports both |
| sizes without records, at a step spanning a knot | refused |
| a grid of times alone, at a step that would substep | refused |
| `Control` | gains `ode_soil_substep_max_uptake`, default 0 |
| odelia 0.7.3 | 0.8.1; plant sets GSL's step-size rule (`Controller::gsl`), which 0.8 no longer defaults to, so no other run's steps move |
| `sum_rainfall`, `sum_infiltration`, `sum_drainage`, `sum_resource_depletion` | unchanged: still the main step's, so they miss rain the soil takes between stages; a run whose water budget must close keeps its stops |

References: only the seasonal regime of `reference-gradient.tsv` moves.
It runs a smooth cubic rain with no stops, so its forward model changed.
{{REFS}} The four constant-rain regimes are bit-identical.
