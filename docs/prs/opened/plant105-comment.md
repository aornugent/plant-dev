### The uptake fraction
`Control$ode_soil_substep_max_uptake` is a fraction. At the start of each step the patch computes the plants' uptake as a fraction of all the water moving through the soil: Σ|uptake| / Σ(|inflow| + |drainage| + |uptake|), summed over the layers. Below the control's value the soil barely feels the plants, so it can be integrated on its own with their uptake extrapolated over the step. The default, 0, leaves only the rain's knots as a reason to substep.

- Across four rainfall records, substeps at the knots cost 0.73–0.90 of the rows of stopping at every knot, and move no quantity by more than 0.23ε.

- Explicit steps on the coupled soil made the reverse sweep unstable on long drought: elasticities up to 2186 where a central difference gives 60.36. With the soil substepped they read 60.37, and the run's CPU falls from 158 s to 77 s.

### What it costs
- Lines: +265/−117 by hand outside the generated RcppR6 and RcppExports files; tests +230/−16.
- Each run's records grow by an optional entry per step, about 0.4 MB per 7 000 steps.

That regime runs a cubic rain with no stops, so its forward model changed: it now reads the rain between stages. The worst row moved 0.32 of its metric's largest column (`theta` on `leaf_area`). ### For review
1. `step_by` refuses a row without substeps that spans a knot. A step substepped only because of the uptake fraction has no such check (a replay at other parameters may compute a different fraction), and NEWS says to carry the records then.
2. `make_node_schedule`'s events path still turns a program into a grid, as on develop; with records it is now refused at a substepping step rather than run coupled.
3. The default uptake fraction of 0 leaves only the knot trigger active. That trigger changes every run on variable rain without stops (the seasonal re-capture); runs that keep their knot stops are unchanged.
