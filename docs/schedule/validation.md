# Validating the schedule: the bank, ε, references, the acceptance suite and the error modes

**Status.** A record of how the refine loop (`refine.md`) and the time axis (`time-axis.md`) were
measured, and what a test in plant must assert. The acceptance suite is designed, not built. Every
reference run the evidence was scored against is lost (§ References), so the first job of any
re-measurement is to rebuild them.

**Sources.** Paths like `measurements/...`, `rootcause/...`, `design-*.md` and `plan-*.md` are on
the tag `archive/trusting-curie`, under `docs/` (rootcause is
`docs/measurements/schedule-search/rootcause/`):
<https://github.com/aornugent/plant-dev/tree/archive/trusting-curie/docs>. `OBJECTIVES.md` is at
this repository's root. Harness paths (`harness/...`) are on the tag unless this repository has
them.

## The bank

**The records** are generated by `harness/long_drought.R` (`RAIN_SPECS`, `rain_record`). Every one
spans 41 years at seed 31 unless stated, and the stand lives 40 years, one species at lma 0.32.

| Record | Definition |
|---|---|
| long-drought | mean 3.0, a seasonal amplitude of 0.8, three multi-year droughts (years 8–10, 19–22, 32–34) and four wet years |
| long-wet | long drought's occurrence pattern at mean 5.0, without the droughts |
| dry | long drought without its droughts, at mean 0.4: near the lowest mean at which the resident's J stays above 1 |
| dry-flat | dry without its season (amplitude 0) |

### The trait-move caveat

Every case's move multiplies one raw parameter after the strategy is built
(`harness/run_record.R`'s `moved()`; `refine_case.R` multiplies `p$strategies[[1]]$pars`). So
`lma ×1.25` leaves the seven parameters TF24 derives from lma at lma 0.32's values. That is the
move a gradient's partial makes, not the move plant's own trait moves make
(`rootcause/review/code/review.md`, Q3). The evidence and its references are consistent with each
other, but a "lma ×1.25" case is not a resident with lma 0.40. The review's default: keep raw
moves until the references are rebuilt the other way.

## ε, and how sure it is

From `measurements/eps-spread.md` (`harness/schedule/data/eps.csv`):
- eight records of the long-drought spec that differ only in seed (31, 101–107), uniform 108
  nodes, tol 1e-4, on the v12-targets build (before PLANT-98);
- ε is a tenth of the sd across the eight, for ln J (0.0252, used as 0.025) and for each
  resident and invader elasticity; never under 0.01 (`OBJECTIVES.md`). `S_D` and `a_f3` have no
  spread by construction and take 0.01;
- `lma` there is the partial in lma alone, not through TF24's hyperparameterisation.

**Uncertainty.** An sd from eight records has 7 degrees of freedom: its 90% interval runs from 0.71
to 1.80 times the estimate. Every "within ε" in the record carries that.

**Caveats.** ε is long drought's spread applied to every record. It was measured on an old build at
108 uniform nodes. Neither has been re-checked.

## References

**What a reference is.** Each case's converged answer, extrapolated from uniform rungs, never seen
by the loop.
- *The held-out recipe* (`rootcause/heldout/notes.md`): u215 and u429 at 3e-5, the case's weights,
  splits and soil share, gradient on. The answer is u429 + (u429 − u215)/3. Its own move,
  (u429 − u215)/3 per quantity in ε, must be under 0.15ε.
- *The rule the refine scoring used* (`rootcause/refine/lib.R`, `ref_ladder`): u429 → u857 where
  both exist; else the bank's lib19-class pair; else u215 → u429.

**How good they were.**
- Five of the second held-out set moved 0.07–0.14ε on their own. flat-al1125, wet-lma08 and
  wet-hmat125 moved 0.18, 0.28 and 0.30ε, all on leaf quantities. flat-al1125's u857 confirmed its
  reference (order 1.91, shift 0.11ε). The others' third rungs were stopped, so wet-lma08 and
  wet-hmat125 carry an unchecked 0.1–0.3ε on `a` and `a_l1`.
- frontier.md: dry-lma125 (u429 → u857) 0.04ε, ld104-p50125 0.07ε, episodic 0.02ε.
- The first held-out set's u215 → u429 references moved 0.10–0.24ε.
- Lumped and spread builds agree within 0.084ε on grids that reach ε, a floor on what any score
  resolves (`design-schedule-building.md`).
- On episodic-lma, errors under about 0.3ε are within the reference's own error (libAG,
  `error-modes.md` H).

**They are lost.** Every run file lived in the session's scratchpad (`/tmp/claude-0/...`), which no
longer exists. Only the summary numbers in the notes survive. The references were also built with
the splits on, and mostly with the spread, both dropped on 19 October. So any re-measurement of
the loop's constants must first rebuild the references on the current stack, with the recipe
above. A u429 rung's forward costs about 4.3 u108 forwards and a u857 rung's about 8.8, each
plus its sweep (`design-schedule-building/verdict.md`; u857 extrapolated there).

## The acceptance suite

Distilled from `design-grid-controller.md` (*The acceptance suite*) and `plan-schedule-search.md`
(C1–C4), **amended** where the refine loop changed what an estimate is.

**Three rules keep it from fixing today's design in place.**
1. It asserts the objectives' bounds and nothing else: error ≤ ε, nudges ≤ ε/3, the bound covers
   the error, no throws, and rows no more than brute force's at the same error. Never a step count,
   a binding component, which rule fired, or a pinned J.
2. Its reference is brute force, not today's controller: one converged answer per fixture,
   regenerated only when plant's scientific surface (its model version) changes.
3. Each fixture's ε is set as OBJECTIVES sets it: a tenth of the spread over a few weather seeds of
   that fixture, computed once with the references, never under 0.01 for an elasticity.

**A fixture** is one record with the resident and invaders at ×0.5 and ×2. Its reference script
also checks that the fixture still shows what is guarded: spell edges, soil-bound steps, a dry
spell long enough to reach the 15-day cap, and invaders at the range's ends. Every run takes both
roles' full gradients.

**The runs:** the refine loop's answer; `tol` ×1.05 and ×0.95 on the loop's schedule; and the
introductions shifted by a quarter spacing.

| Objective | Compared | Bound |
|---|---|---|
| R1, accurate | the corrected answer Q − e against the reference | ≤ ε per quantity |
| R2, reproducible | each nudged run against the base | ≤ ε/3 |
| R4 and R8, honest *(amended)* | the true error of Q − e against the reported bound 0.5\|e\| + u_q | covered for every quantity |
| R3, continuous | two nearby invaders on one recording: their chord against the mean of their gradients | ≤ ε/3 |
| R5, never fails | invaders at the range's ends, the resident at ±10% | no throw; a failed run returns `status = "failed"` with the last complete pass |
| the diagonal | the invader's J′ against J at the resident's traits | bit-equal |
| R7, performant | rows against brute force's at the same achieved error | no more |

**The amendment.** As designed, the suite required the estimate within 0.5–2× of the true error.
The refine loop's replay reads 0.25–0.65 of the error without the spread
(`rootcause/refine2/frontier.md`), so the raw estimate would fail that test while the loop is
honest. What the loop guarantees is coverage: |(Q − e) − ref| ≤ 0.5|e| + u_q. That is what the
suite asserts. The search's C2 (*no silent miss*: every quantity over ε/2 has an estimate over
ε/4, `plan-schedule-search.md`) is dropped for the same reason: at a read of 0.25 an error of
0.5ε gives an estimate of 0.125ε, while the corrected answer is still covered. A read below two thirds near ε is the coefficient's known failure (`refine.md`,
*What this makes hard*) and shows up as a coverage miss.

**Tiers.**
- *Every edit, seconds:* each build's component checks (weights of one change nothing, the adjoint
  against differences, nesting of levels, even counts).
- *Before a push, under 3 minutes serial:* one fixture and the table. That needs a forward of about
  5 s, from fewer nodes and a shorter lifetime or a faster-maturing species, with J still earned
  inside the run. **Tension:** the refine loop at scale 1 puts about 200 nodes on a 40-year stand;
  the suite was designed for 20–27. A short fixture needs its own spell structure and its own ε.
- *Nightly or before a merge, about 15 minutes:* three fixtures (pulsed, wet, constant), one level
  against the next, and a seeded sweep of invader traits. A trait point that ever fails joins a
  list rerun every time.
- *Milestones, hours:* the bank.

**An objective not yet met is skipped with its reason, not dropped.** A build is accepted when its
skip comes out and the test passes. Today's skips: R1 for curvatures and invaders (not built);
R2 at 1e-4 on near-extinction cases (dry-lma125's 0.42ε nudge, `time-axis.md`).

**`tol` stands in for the target** until the controller takes an accuracy target.

**Cost:** about 300 lines in plant's tests (a fixture helper, the property tests, the reference
script), with no dependence on plant-dev's harness. References take 1–3 CPU hours once.

**Rejected:** pinned-output snapshots (broken by every controller improvement), and the bank alone
(the right checks, at hours a run).

**How the record ran a test.** Every round wrote its predictions with a probability before any run
(e.g. frontier.md's F1–F15, 10 of 16 held), scored errors in each quantity's ε against references
the loop never saw, compared CPU only between interleaved runs under the same load, and
reported rows as the load-free measure. A future round should do the same.

**The record's own reading** (`error-modes.md`, *Reading for the user's argument*): the layout's
and the estimator's modes (A, B1–B2, C1–C5) are fixed once the rule is fixed, and each was designed
out by changing the rule, not by reading a run. What is left is the step-sequence jitter (D1–D3),
the stiff soil (E2), throws (F) and the case and θ (G1–G3). G1 is the one mode a check at θ₀ cannot
cover.

**Its open questions that still bear on the loop:** whether an edge inside an interval costs
anything by itself (question 1); invaders, untested throughout (9); d*'s value (10); sub-ε stiff-soil
contamination under the soil substeps (11); the late square law past 110 d (12); constant rain (13);
water-balance sums 3.1% short of the rain on dry under libS (15).
