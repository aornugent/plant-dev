What one leaf placement costs, at the current triple.

Taken with phylloptim/tests/cpp/probe_tape_regions (statements and operations,
exact) and valgrind --tool=callgrind over its `placements` mode (instructions,
exact). One species, an Interior operating point.

⚠️ THREE QUANTITIES, AND THEY DISAGREE ON PURPOSE. Statements are the tape's
bookkeeping. Operations are the arithmetic recorded under them. Instructions are
what the machine pays, and they alone see arithmetic that never reaches a tape --
which is all of a tangent's. Price a change by all three: the first two say where
the tape's cost is, the third where the arithmetic's is, and neither alone has
been enough here twice over.

⚠️ AND A STATEMENT'S PRICE DEPENDS ON WHERE IT IS. A permanent statement is
recorded once and swept once per census metric, of which this stand seeds three.
A statement inside an implicit_value residual is recorded, swept once locally,
and rewound. So the same arithmetic costs about three sweeps outside a residual
and one inside it -- which is why hoisting a common subexpression out of one can
lose.

⚠️ FIVE LAYERS IS THE STAND'S. Environment("TF24") reports
get_soil_number_of_depths() == 5, and the supply draw scales with them: 17
statements a placement at one layer, 73 at five. A one-layer probe understates
the largest block in the leaf four-fold.

⚠️ THIS DRIVER SWEEPS ONCE AND THE STAND SWEEPS THREE TIMES. It builds one
recording and takes one adjoint pass, so every permanent statement here is
charged a third of what the stand charges it. Read this for RECORDING and
values/century-profile.txt for the whole cost.

⚠️ AND IT BUILDS THE SPLINES ONCE FOR SIXTY PLACEMENTS WHERE THE STAND BUILDS
THEM ONCE PER LEAF. The incomplete gamma at 11.48% is the fixture's setup, not a
placement's cost. Anything named here has to be checked against the stand's
profile before it is called a share of the run.
