What one leaf placement costs, at the current triple.

Taken with phylloptim/tests/cpp/probe_tape_regions (statements and operations,
exact) and valgrind --tool=callgrind over a 60-placement driver (instructions,
exact). One species, one soil layer, an Interior operating point.

⚠️ THREE QUANTITIES, AND THEY DISAGREE ON PURPOSE. Statements are the tape's
bookkeeping. Operations are the arithmetic recorded under them. Instructions are
what the machine pays, and they alone see arithmetic that never reaches a tape --
which is all of a tangent's. A change is priced by the third; the first two say
where to look.

WHAT THE TOP TWO ARE. Neither depends on ci, and both sit inside a ci-derivative:
assim_colimited_kernel recomputes vcmax, the electron transport and the dark
respiration on every call, and kernel_slope_at differentiates it in n_pars + 1
directions. That is 34.7% of the boundary spent re-deriving quantities that do
not move with the variable being differentiated. The pre-merge header carried a
five-argument assim_colimited_kernel(ci, vcmax, transport, curv, resp) so a
caller could hoist them; the merge collapsed it into the pars-reading form.
