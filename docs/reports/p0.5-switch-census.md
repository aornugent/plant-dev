## What the numbers mean for a gradient

**The root vulnerability domain edge is the one number here that should change a
decision.** Every other zero in P0.5 is a comfortable zero: the shut-down exits
sit 27x `GSS_tol_abs` away, the light floor needs five times this stand's optical
depth, the soil positivity guard is a factor of 15.6 clear. This one is 1.15x
clear, and the magnitude that gets within 15% of the edge is not a state variable
— it is an iterate of the collar root-find, at 5.92 MPa against an
`|opt_root_psi|` of at most 2.36 MPa at the output times. So the census would
report a much larger margin than the solver actually holds, and a drier driver, a
wider bracket or a looser tolerance moves the iterate, not the state. The
sign-error path is nonetheless narrower than the specification's summary implies:
`root_vuln_from_psi`, the spline that extrapolates negative, is read only in the
equal-potentials branch (1.30% of layer evals), and that branch already stops on
`f_ri <= 0`. The general branch reads the cumulative-integral spline, whose linear
extrapolation is positive, so beyond the edge it produces a wrong conductance
rather than a wrong-sign flux. The row is still owed a fix, because 1.15x is not a
margin, but the fix wanted is a domain assertion, not a second sign guard.

**The establishment gate is the most consequential number in this pass, and it is
crossed at magnitudes indistinguishable from zero.** It fires on 22.43% of
boundary-node stage evaluations, and the `net_mass_production_dt` that puts the
seedling in the closed arm reaches **−2.283012e-09** at its largest — seven orders
of magnitude below the median `|P|` of 7.3e-2 that report 00 §9b measures for the
resident population, and five below `storage_prod_eps = 1e-4`, the scale develop
already uses to smooth the same quantity's positive part one function away. So the
gate is not separating a healthy seedling from a dying one; over the window it
fires in, it is deciding on a quantity that is numerically zero, and
`d(pr_estab)/d(state)` jumps from `0` to the full `1/(tmp^2 + 1)` slope across it.
Two consequences: the whole of `node.h:182-185`'s active set is downstream of this
one switch, so smoothing the gate removes both rows at once; and a
finite-difference verification of any census gradient taken in `t` in [3.2, 8.5]
straddles it, because a perturbation of `1e-9` in net production flips the arm.

**`mortality_dt`'s guard is the largest live severance found in this pass.** At
4.95% of its 7 516 566 calls the rate is replaced by an exact `0.0`, so
`d(mortality)/d(state)` is zero on that set, and every census metric built as
`sum_k exp(l_k) psi(state_k)` inherits it. It is not a rounding effect: 327 of
10 153 records hold `mortality = Inf` outright, so the set is not near a threshold
a mollifier could smooth — the cohort is genuinely at survival zero, and the
honest treatment is to drop the node rather than to zero its derivative and keep
carrying it.

**`species.h:220` is the row where a smoothing scale would be needed and none
exists.** `f_h1 > 0` decides the boundary arm at values as small as
`2.714503e-11`, so the branch is taken and not taken across a range of `f_h1`
spanning eleven orders of magnitude down to where the comparison is deciding on
rounding. The term it switches on is `(h1 - h0) * (f_h1 + f_h0)`, which vanishes
as `f_h1 -> 0` only if `f_h0` vanishes with it, and `f_h0` is the boundary node's
own competition at the query height, which does not. The field reduction is
therefore discontinuous in the resident state at a threshold located at 1e-11, and
it fires on 2.41% of field queries. Compared with the two scales measured in
report 00 §9b — `storage_prod_eps = 1e-4` against a median `|P|` of 7.3e-2, which
is well sized — this construct has no scale at all.

**`species.h:215` is a term count, not a severance, and its 97.18% is reassuring
rather than alarming.** The break fires almost always because the field is queried
well below the canopy top, so the sweep stops after the first node pair that
straddles the query height. The gradient consequence is the one report 00 §8 item
3 names: the trapezium weights are functions of state, and dropping `d/dh` of the
interval widths is an error on 216 594 704 terms rather than on a few.

**`patch.h:568` and `node.h:177` need no derivative treatment.** The first is true
on all 35 274 stage evaluations and the second never takes its closed arm at a
minimum growth rate of 0.095 m/yr. `node.h:144-150` also needs none, but for the
opposite reason: it is dead code at this driver, and the effect it is credited
with belongs to the `exp` underflow one line above it.

**One property of the model shows up in the denominators themselves.** The
boundary node is re-seeded 35 133 times against 141 introductions, and the field
rebuilt 35 274 times — once per species per stage. So `compute_initial_conditions`
runs a full `establishment_probability`, and therefore a full leaf solve at
`height_0` on the shared `Leaf`, 249 times per introduction. That is why every
`node.h` incidence here carries a stage denominator rather than an introduction
one.

Six, in order of exposure:

1. `establishment_probability`'s gate — 22.43% of boundary-node stage
   evaluations, crossed at `net_mass_production_dt` of order 1e-9, no scale.
   P0.6's to decide.

   scale.
4. `species.h:215`'s break — 97.18% of field queries, a state-dependent term
   count whose interval-width derivatives are not carried anywhere.
5. `node.h:182-185`'s `log_density` guard — 22.43% of stage evaluations, entirely
   downstream of item 1, so it needs no treatment of its own if item 1 gets one.
6. the `prev_q == 0` break — 10.16% of loop calls, 33.78% of records; a selector
   in cohort height that changes how many layers receive roots, on the direct
   `h -> water` path.

The only zero that still needs something recorded is the root vulnerability domain
edge — a domain assertion, at a 1.15x margin. The remaining zeros are structural.
