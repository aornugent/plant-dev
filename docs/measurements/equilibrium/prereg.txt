The stand at its demographic equilibrium (pre-registered 2026-10-05, before any
run). Every number measured so far on TF24 was taken at a birth rate of 1,
where the long-drought stand's J is 12.67: about thirteen times what holds it
steady. An evolutionary analysis runs at b*, where J(b*) = b*, and reaches b* by
repeating the run. This measures what that costs and what a run at b* is.

Setting: long drought, its own seed, 40 years, lma 0.32, 108 uniform
introductions held, steps adapting at 1e-4 with the absolute tolerance tied
(ATOL 1e-4), the birth-date coordinate, splits at sign changes on. Built from
odelia ODELIA-54 373f5b9, plant PLANT-103 4f45b702, phylloptim PHYLLOPTIM-17.
Run alone: harness/equilibrium.R by run.sh beside this file.

What runs:
- the equilibrium by a secant on f(x) = ln J(e^x) - x, x = ln b, from b = 1 and
  the fixed point's first step, to |f| < 1e-5 (regnans's equilibrium_eps);
- b <- J(b), regnans's default equilibrium_iteration, for eight runs from b = 1
  (the secant's first two included);
- at b*: the stand recorded and swept, the invader at the stand's traits walked
  at birth rate 1 and swept, and five invaders over lma x e^{-0.1..0.1} walked
  together, then swept together;
- lma moved by 1% alone, run at b* and at b* moved by the implicit function
  theorem's d ln b*/d ln lma = -(d ln J/d ln lma)/(f'(x*)), the elasticity from
  the stand's sweep and f' from the secant's last two runs.

Predictions:
P1. The secant reaches |f| < 1e-5 in at most 8 runs.
P2. b <- J(b) contracts by m = d ln J/d ln b at b* each run, and needs at least
    twice the secant's runs to reach 1e-5, counted or extrapolated from m.
P3. A run at b* costs within 30% of a run at b = 1 (68-71 s).
P4. The five-invader walk costs 4-6 times the one-invader walk.
P5. The invader at the stand's traits has J' = J(b*)/b* to 1e-6 relative.
P6. The implicit function theorem's move leaves |f| at most a tenth of the
    held birth rate's.
P7. The stand's sweep costs at most 4 forwards and the invader's at most 3.5 of
    its walks, as at b = 1 (3.6 and 3.2). The five-invader sweep costs at least
    4 single-invader sweeps (no saving from batching invaders in one sweep).

Extension: regnans's own root-finders (pre-registered 2026-10-05, after the
secant and the iteration above had run and before these did). regnans offers
nleqslv (Broyden, global "none", its starting Jacobian by its own differences)
and dfsane on ln b, each with the target J/b - 1, at tol 1e-5 from b = 1.
harness/regnans_solvers.R sources regnans's util_nlsolve.R and the target from
R/community_demography.R at traitecoevo/regnans 56ad241, and drives them with
the same runner and setting as above, stopping either after 25 runs. Run alone,
after the runs above, by run_solvers.sh.

P8. nleqslv reaches tol 1e-5 from b = 1 within 10 runs, the first (regnans's
    check that the species is kept) included.
P9. dfsane reaches tol 1e-5 from b = 1 within 15 runs.

Extension: the stand at b* replayed on its own program (pre-registered with the
root-finders, before either ran). harness/equilibrium_replay.R runs the stand at
b* adaptively, replays its recorded program (p$ode_times with p$ode_step_sizes)
at b*, then runs lma moved by 1% alone both ways, at b* held.

P10. The replay at b* costs at most 0.85 of the adaptive run and reproduces its
     J to 1e-12 relative.
P11. At lma x 1.01 the replay's ln J and the adaptive run's differ by less than
     a tenth of epsilon/3 (8.4e-4): one grid serves a 1% move at b*.
