Runs: one each, in that order, with nothing else on the machine.

included, against the floor's; ln J against the floor's in eps; the stack's 49

develop's ln J against the floor's and, computed not run, the cost of its
gradient by central differences, 2 forwards an elasticity.

1e-5 it carries its own node and time error, measured within eps only on wet
and dry.
Expected: the stack within eps/3 of the floor on most entries and within eps on
all, for 0.15-0.3 of its time; develop's forward of the same order as the
stack's, its J off the floor by the coordinate's node error, and its gradient
