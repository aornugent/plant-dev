Pathfinder: can an invader's walk and sweep drop members by their own share of J'?
Pre-registered 2026-10-03, before any run or emulation in pf_thin/.

HYPOTHESIS
H0. A rare invader moves no field: its walk follows the resident's recorded
    field and steps, and its members interact only with that field. So dropping
    invader members (a subset of the run's introductions) changes J' only through
    the dropped members' own contribution and through the birth-date quadrature
    of the merged intervals; there is no field part (unlike the resident's
    thinning after b = 10, grid-dynamics.md section 8 test 2).

Testable predictions:
P1 (no coupling, step 2). On a real thinned walk every kept member's
    fecundity, and each merged interval's establishment integral and moment,
    equal the full walk's sums to rounding (relative 1e-12), so J' equals the
    offline emulation to 1e-12 relative.
P2 (rule a, step 1). Dropping members born after the cumulative J' share
    reaches 1 - delta, delta = 1e-3 and 1e-4, moves ln J' by less than eps/3.
P3 (rule b, step 1). Spacing members as (share density)^(-1/3) (root law),
    with one constant for every invader and record, moves ln J' by less than
    eps/3 and saves more rows than rule (a) at matched error.

SETUP
- Library DEV/lib_sw (odelia state-weights, phylloptim 378b083, plant
  state-weights 4555ea13). Bounded Cash-Karp: TOL=3e-5 ATOL=1e-4 WEIGHT_SOIL=10
  HMAX=15 WEIGHT_MAX=100 WEIGHT=window/rule_A/weight_<record>.rds
  TIMES=window/t/t_u108.rds, records long-drought, long-wet, episodic.
- Invaders: the stand's own (theta' = theta, the diagonal) and lma, hmat at
  x0.5, 0.7, 1.4, 2: 9 per record, 27 cases.
- Step 1 data: re-run each record's stand (record_trajectory) and its 9 walks
  (no sweeps), keeping per node: birth b_j, establishment weight w_j, the
  interval's establishment integral E_j and moment M_j (node ODE states
  interval_establishment, interval_establishment_moment), fecundity, patch
  density at birth, S_D and birth rate. Check the re-run reproduces
  phase1c/combined/full/bnd_*.rds (J, each J') bit for bit.
- Emulation (exact, by construction of species.h for_each_establishment_weight):
  J'(S) = sum_{j in S} wt_j F_j, F_j = fecundity_j * patch_density_j * S_D * br_j;
  merged interval a..c: E = sum E_k, M = sum (M_k + (b_k - b_a) E_k); the last
  kept node's right interval runs to the run's end, with the boundary node's
  share dropped as plant drops it. Checked: emulated J'(all) = reported J'.
- Rows: per accepted step, the kept members born at or before its start
  (bounded_table.R's rows_of). Walk and sweep both scale with rows; report the
  fraction of the invader's rows saved.
- eps: ln J 0.025 (eps/3 = 0.0083, OBJECTIVES). Elasticities: invader eps from
  docs/measurements/eps.csv, floored at 0.01 (OBJECTIVES); S_D, a_f3 take 0.01.

CRITERIA
- Go to step 2 if one rule setting, fixed across all 27 cases (not tuned per
  case), saves a median >= 30% of the invader's walk+sweep rows with every
  case's |d ln J'| < eps/3 on the exact emulation. Otherwise stop and report why.
- Step 2 (real thinned walks and sweeps on long drought and episodic), pass if:
  (i) P1 holds; (ii) |d ln J'| < eps/3 for every invader; (iii) every invader
  elasticity moves < eps/3 (floored eps) against the full walk's; (iv) walk +
  sweep time falls >= 30% (median over invaders). The diagonal (stand's own
  invader) is reported separately: the full walk has J' = J to the bit.
- Refutation of H0: P1 fails (kept members' values move beyond rounding).
- Refutation of the lever: no fixed setting meets the go criterion, or step 2's
  gradients move >= eps/3 at the setting that meets it.
