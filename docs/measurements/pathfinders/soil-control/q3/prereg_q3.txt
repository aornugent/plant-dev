EXPLORATORY VARIANT mrw, written 21:06 UTC before it ran (not eligible to pass Q3).
  Mechanism read from the first runs: on a flagged step the members' stages read the
  predictor's soil, and the only estimate of that soil's error, |theta_C - theta_P| (with the
  defect), is judged at the soil's weight, 10 x the members' (bounded at 100). So the members
  may read a soil ten times further off than their own error budget allows, and nothing sees it.
  mrw = mr with that estimate judged at the members' weight instead: multiplied by
  min(10 f, 100) / min(f, 100), f rule A's factor at the step's start (harness_w/, MR_COUPLE=member;
  unflagged steps unchanged).
  Hypothesis: mrw's |J/J* - 1| falls to within max(2 x bnd's, 0.01 eps) on both records, with
  rows still <= 0.9 x bnd's. Refuted if J's error stays above 0.5 x mr's on either record (the
  bias then lies elsewhere: the extrapolated uptake, or the stage soil itself).
  Runs: mrw_ld, mrw_epi (forward, 3e-5); elasticities only if the hypothesis holds.
