Pre-registration: step 2's gates, splits in plant's forward step
Written before any run of this test (2026-10-04).

The question
  Does the split built into odelia's step and plant's patch (design item 2) meet
  its gates on long drought? The R driver's split met them on the same setting
  (grid-dynamics.md section 11 and section 17); this asks whether the build does.

The build
  odelia and plant on branch sign-changes, over state-weights (odelia a62e97c,
  plant 4555ea13), with phylloptim09, in one private library. A part is a node;
  it is split where its net production changes sign inside a step the error
  estimate keeps, on Cash-Karp's fourth-order dense output.

The setting
  harness/run_record.R, FORWARD=1: long drought (seed 31), 108 uniform nodes,
  the tied tolerance (ATOL=1e-4). SPLIT=1 turns the split on; LMA_REL=r runs the
  stand at lma (1 + r); PROGRAM pins the steps to a run's own.

The runs
  A. tol 1e-3, 3e-4, 1e-4, 3e-5 and 1e-5, plain and split.
  B. References: split at 1e-6 and 1e-7, plain at 1e-7.
  C. Cost: plain and split at 1e-4 again, alternating, alone on the machine.
  D. Each arm's own program at 1e-4 replayed at lma (1 + r), r in +-1e-3,
     +-1e-2 and +-3e-2, each arm with its own split setting.

The quantities
  J's error: |J - J_ref| / J_ref, J_ref the split run at 1e-7.
  Cost: the stand_run phase's seconds, split over plain, the median of two.
  H(r): the second difference of ln J in ln lma about lma (uscan.R's), in eps
  for lma's curvature in the resident role, 1.17.

The predictions
  P1 (J's error falls with tol). The split's error falls at every rung from 1e-3
     to 1e-5, and at 1e-4 it is at most the plain arm's.
  P2 (the references). The split and the plain arm at 1e-7 agree within 1e-6 of
     J, and the split at 1e-6 is within 1e-7 of the split at 1e-7.
  P3 (cost). At 1e-4 the split's forward costs at most 6% more than the plain.
  P4 (the curvature). The split's H at r = 1e-2 and 3e-2 differ by less than
     eps/10, and each is within eps/3 of the driver's converged -43.45
     (prereg_uscan.txt, P5).
  P5 (passage). The split's H at r = 1e-3 is within eps/3 of its H at 1e-2; the
     plain arm's is not (the record has it 2.8-4.4 eps off at r up to 1e-3).

What decides
  - P1, P3 and P4 hold: step 2 meets its gates; build the sweep (item 3).
  - P1 fails: the split does not make J's time error follow tol in the build,
    though it did in the driver; find what differs before item 3.
  - P3 fails: price where the time goes (locating, pieces, the corrected end,
    the extra copies of the state) before item 3.
  - P4 or P5 fails with P1 holding: the curvature carries something the
    forward's error does not; the pair of sign changes inside one step is the
    first candidate.
  Not measured here: a pair of sign changes merging, and halving only the split
  steps; both need each step's split marked, which nothing records.

Runs: run.sh. Analysis: analyze.R.

Second extension: the passage hypothesis. Written after the lma-alone replays
(the split's H -42.862, -43.438, -43.468 at r = 1e-3, 1e-2, 3e-2) and before the
probe below was built or run (2026-10-04).
  The build repeats the driver's scan (-42.89, -43.44, -43.47), pairs detected or
  not, so the residue below r = 1e-2 is not the pair of sign changes inside one
  step. The hypothesis: each sign change that passes from one step to the next
  moves J by about 5e-9, with one sign. A piece reads the field from the dense
  output where the unsplit step read it from its stages, so a cut at either end
  of a step does not reduce to the unsplit step. With N(r) passages, about 78,
  200 and 650 at r = 1e-3, 3e-3 and 1e-2, the residue is N(r) 5e-9 / r^2: 0.33,
  0.095 and 0.028 eps, against 0.47 here at 1e-3 and the driver's 0.094 and 0.026.
  The probe (odelia sign-changes-diff, local): the step's own end for a split
  part is corrected by what the cuts change, the pieces less the whole step, both
  in the dense output's field, so a cut at either end changes nothing. It costs
  five more part evaluations a split.
  Runs: its forward at 1e-4, and replays of its own program at lma alone,
  r = +-1e-3, +-1e-2, +-3e-2 (diff_*).
  P6. The probe's H at r = 1e-3 is within eps/10 of its H at 1e-2.
  P7. Its H at 1e-2 and 3e-2 stays within eps/3 of -43.45, and its J at 1e-4 is
      no farther from J_ref than twice the split's.
  P6 failing refutes the hypothesis; P6 holding with P7 failing says the
  correction removes the passages but biases the curvature.

     5.76e-6, beside plain's 5.27e-6: both arms carry the same error there.
  P2: the split and plain agree at 1e-7 within 2.5e-7, and the driver's J* within

     both arms share falls about 5x from 1e-5 to 1e-6; the kinks do not carry it.
  P3 fails narrowly: alone on the machine, the split's forward at 1e-4 costs
     6.9% more than plain's (73.7 and 77.6 s against 70.3 and 71.3 s), 0.53 ms a
     node step split. The two split runs differ by 5% between themselves.
     Extension: the split at 1e-4 costs 0.77 of plain at 1e-5.
  P4 holds: lma alone, the split's H is -43.438 at r = 1e-2 and -43.468 at 3e-2,
     0.026 eps apart and within 0.015 eps of -43.45. Plain: -44.004, -43.622.
  P5 fails: from r = 1e-2 to 1e-3 the split's H moves 0.49 eps (bar 1/3), plain's
     2.72 eps. The build repeats the driver's scan at every r it shares.

  The harness's replays step to the program's times: with events, plant's

Third extension: what the probe leaves. Written after the probe's verdicts and
one bisection, before the scan below was built or run (2026-10-04).
  The bisection (diag/bisect.sh, run before this entry; its expectation, a jump
  of 1e-8 to 1e-7 in ln J, was stated in the session and not here) narrowed node
  1's passage from step 83 to step 84, between r = -1e-3 and 0, to 9.5e-10 in r.
  Across that bracket ln J moves -1.64e-8 where the smooth change is -0.79e-8:
  node 1's cut at u = 1 - 3e-9 in step 83 ceasing moves ln J by -0.85e-8.
  It also found a window, at most 3.2e-8 wide in r, where node 1's sign change is
  split in neither step. Step 83 decides on its end's sign values before the
  parts it splits (nodes 3 to 7) are corrected, and the end is rated again after;
  node 1's value, within 1e-6 of zero, changes sign in between, so step 84 starts
  on the far side. The window divides a passage's jump in two and adds nothing
  to it.
  The hypothesis: what the probe leaves is carried by pairs of sign changes the
  detection misses. It looks for a pair only through a stage strictly inside the
  step that holds the other sign, and those stages sit at u = 0.2, 0.3, 0.6 and
  0.875, so a pair inside (0, 0.2), (0.3, 0.6), (0.6, 0.875) or (0.875, 1) goes
  unsplit, and a pair moving across a stage is split or not with a jump of all
  the unsplit step lost in it. Between r = 0 and +-1e-3 six pairs (nodes 7, 10,
  24, 70 and 74, and node 5's across steps 270 and 271) are split at one r and
  not the other, 0.08 to 0.22 of a step wide: too wide to have opened or closed
  within 1e-3 in lma.
  The scan (odelia sign-changes-log, local, ODELIA_SPLIT_SCAN set): after each
  kept step, P on the dense output at u = k/32 for every part, logging each part
  whose number of sign changes there differs from its number of cuts. Runs:
  replays of the split's program at r = 0 and +-1e-3 (scan_*).
  P8. Each of the six pairs shows on the scan at the r where it was not split,
      as two changes more than its cuts.
  P9. Missed pairs are common: at r = 0 the scan finds at least 20 node steps
      with a pair the detection missed, and fewer than 5 where the detection cut
      a pair the scan does not show.
  Then the fix probe (odelia sign-changes-scan, local): cut wherever the 32-point
  scan shows a change, over the probe's correction. Its forward at 1e-4 and
  replays of its own program at r = +-1e-3, +-1e-2, +-3e-2 (fix_*).
  P10. Its H at r = 1e-3 is within eps/10 of its H at 1e-2.
  P8 failing refutes the hypothesis (the pairs open and close rather than hide
  between stages). P8 holding with P10 failing says the misses are real but
  something else carries the rest.

Fourth extension: the noise's source (2026-10-04, written before the build and
the replays; tight.sh, tight.R, tight_phylloptim.patch, tight_plant.patch)
  Read again (noise.R, noise.log), every replay on a pinned grid carries noise
  of about 1.9e-8 in ln J, plain too, under a change of lma as small as 1e-12.
  Hypothesis: it comes from iterations that stop on a tolerance, whose result
  moves by up to that tolerance when an ulp upstream flips one of their
  comparisons: the leaf solve's root-finds (TOMS748 at 1e-4 for the root's
  potential, 1e-10 for ci, 1e-12 for the collar; bisection at ci_abs_tol), its
  searches (Brent at ci_abs_tol or 1e-4 of the interval, golden section at
  GSS_tol_abs), and plant's own (the newborn's height, bisection at 1e-8).
  Test: plant (abcfcc22, plain) built against a phylloptim (378b083) whose
  root-finds stop only once their bracket is under 1e-15 + 4e-16 of its ends,
  adjacent floats, and whose two searches stop at 1e-12, with plant's own
  root-finds stopping alike. Decision thresholds stay as they are: the collar
  interval's collapse at GSS_tol_abs, and the steps inside it until the
  gradient is real. The plain program (program_plain.rds) is replayed at
  lma (1 + k 1e-12), k = -3..3, on the tight build, and at k = +-2, +-3 on the
  split build, whose k = 0, +-1 exist.
  Measure: on each build, the six changes of ln J between neighbouring k less
  the smooth change (-8.277e-12 a step), and their rms.
  T1, the tolerances carry the noise: the tight build's rms is at most a tenth
     of the split build's. T1 fails if it is half or more; between the two, the
     tolerances carry part of it.
  Reported, not judged: ln J at k = 0 on each build, which is what the
  tightening moves J by, and each replay's time.

Result of the fourth extension (tight.log): T1 holds. Plain's changes between
  neighbouring k fall from 2.7e-8 rms on the split build to 1.1e-14 on the
  tight build, a ratio of 4e-7; ln J at k = 0 moves by -1.5e-6.
