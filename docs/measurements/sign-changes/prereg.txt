Pre-registration: step 2's gates, splits in plant's forward step
Written before any run of this test (2026-10-04).

The question
  Does the split built into odelia's step and plant's patch (design item 2) meet
  its gates on long drought? The R driver's split met them on the same setting
  (grid-dynamics.md section 11 and section 17); this asks whether the build does.

The build
  odelia and plant on branch sign-changes, over state-weights (odelia a62e97c,
  plant 4555ea13), with phylloptim09, in one private library. A part is a node;
  it is split where its net production changes sign inside a step the error
  estimate keeps, on Cash-Karp's fourth-order dense output.

The setting
  harness/run_record.R, FORWARD=1: long drought (seed 31), 108 uniform nodes,
  the tied tolerance (ATOL=1e-4). SPLIT=1 turns the split on; LMA_REL=r runs the
  stand at lma (1 + r); PROGRAM pins the steps to a run's own.

The runs
  A. tol 1e-3, 3e-4, 1e-4, 3e-5 and 1e-5, plain and split.
  B. References: split at 1e-6 and 1e-7, plain at 1e-7.
  C. Cost: plain and split at 1e-4 again, alternating, alone on the machine.
  D. Each arm's own program at 1e-4 replayed at lma (1 + r), r in +-1e-3,
     +-1e-2 and +-3e-2, each arm with its own split setting.

The quantities
  J's error: |J - J_ref| / J_ref, J_ref the split run at 1e-7.
  Cost: the stand_run phase's seconds, split over plain, the median of two.
  H(r): the second difference of ln J in ln lma about lma (uscan.R's), in eps
  for lma's curvature in the resident role, 1.17.

The predictions
  P1 (J's error falls with tol). The split's error falls at every rung from 1e-3
     to 1e-5, and at 1e-4 it is at most the plain arm's.
  P2 (the references). The split and the plain arm at 1e-7 agree within 1e-6 of
     J, and the split at 1e-6 is within 1e-7 of the split at 1e-7.
  P3 (cost). At 1e-4 the split's forward costs at most 6% more than the plain.
  P4 (the curvature). The split's H at r = 1e-2 and 3e-2 differ by less than
     eps/10, and each is within eps/3 of the driver's converged -43.45
     (prereg_uscan.txt, P5).
  P5 (passage). The split's H at r = 1e-3 is within eps/3 of its H at 1e-2; the
     plain arm's is not (the record has it 2.8-4.4 eps off at r up to 1e-3).

What decides
  - P1, P3 and P4 hold: step 2 meets its gates; build the sweep (item 3).
  - P1 fails: the split does not make J's time error follow tol in the build,
    though it did in the driver; find what differs before item 3.
  - P3 fails: price where the time goes (locating, pieces, the corrected end,
    the extra copies of the state) before item 3.
  - P4 or P5 fails with P1 holding: the curvature carries something the
    forward's error does not; the pair of sign changes inside one step is the
    first candidate.
  Not measured here: a pair of sign changes merging, and halving only the split
  steps; both need each step's split marked, which nothing records.

Runs: run.sh. Analysis: analyze.R.
