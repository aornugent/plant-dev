# The constant front against traits (registered before the runs)

Claim (docs/dynamics.md): the front is where a cohort's net production first
grazes zero (sign-change count 0 -> 2). Its position is set by the compensation
and race axes; its strength by the buffer axis.

Runs: constant rain, nr setting, lib_109, forward only, full lifetime. pA's nodes
plus a node every 1/16 day over days 12-45. Traits moved one at a time from the
stand's own: lma x0.8, x1.25; hmat x0.8, x1.25 (compensation and race); a_dG1
x0.5, x2; a_st1 x0.5, x2 (buffer). Front b* = the first birth whose sign-change
count exceeds the count of the births before it; its strength = the rise in
cumulative mortality to the run's end over the 0.25 day after b*.

Predictions:
1. |b*(lma) - b*(base)| and |b*(hmat) - b*(base)| each exceed 0.25 day for at
   least one of the two moves of each trait.
2. The buffer moves shift b* by less than the larger of the lma and hmat shifts.
3. Strength rises with a_dG1 (x2 above base above x0.5) and falls with a_st1
   (x0.5 above base above x2).
