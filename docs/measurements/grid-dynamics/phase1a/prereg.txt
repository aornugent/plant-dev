  An accumulator run (TOL_ACC = TOL_SOIL, suffix "a") is added for a variant whose
  accumulators bind >= 2% of its accepted steps at 3e-5 (baseline 0.1%). The accumulators
  are diagnostics never consumed by the dynamics, so weighting them cannot move J.

SCORES (every run)
  rows.R against DEV/seed/tied_3e-5.rds: accepted, rows, forward member evaluations,
    gradient-run cost (forward + 6 rows)/7.
  e_J = J/J* - 1.
  Soil error: at every time common to the run's accepted steps and the tight monolithic
    recording DEV/partition_bias/out/rec_1e-7.rds (CK, tied, 1e-7, 34 301 steps; the
    knots and introductions are step targets in both), d = |theta_run - theta_ref| per
    layer. Reported: max over times and layers (soil_max), and the median over common
    times of the max over layers (soil_med).
  Binding components of the accepted steps (the driver's "binding:" line).

CONVERGENCE (R4), per variant, on e_J and on each of soil_max and soil_med:
  (a) |x(1e-5)| <= max(|x(1e-4)| / 3, floor), and
  (b) |x(3e-5)| <= 1.5 * max(|x(1e-4)|, floor).
  floor: 6e-6 for e_J (the tied runs at 1e-5 and 1e-6 sit at +5.2e-6 and -6.1e-6 of J*,
  so below that the reference cannot rank them); 0 for the soil.
  A variant failing (a) or (b) on e_J or on soil_max is "not converging".

SURVIVOR (stage 1 -> stage 2): completes all three tolerances without raising, rows at
  3e-5 below the baseline's 962 505, and converging. If more survive than the budget
  allows, stage 2 takes the explicit survivor with the fewest rows and the implicit
  survivor with the fewest rows, the fewest-rows survivor first.

PASS (stage 2)
  R2: for every main quantity measured, the largest |move| over the nudge triple
    (x0.95 and x1.05 against x1) < eps/3.
  R1: |Q(3e-5) - Q_ref| < eps for every main quantity measured. Q_ref:
    ln J -> ln J*;
    plant-measured elasticities -> plant's tied CK run at 1e-5 on the same 108 nodes
      (docs/measurements/nudges/ld_1e-5.rds);
    frozen-difference a_dG1, a_dG2, d_I (partial elasticities) -> the same ld_1e-5.rds;
    frozen-difference lma -> the baseline triple's frozen lma (mean of the three).
  Errors falling with the tolerance: stage 1's convergence on e_J and the soil.
  Winner: the passing variant with the fewest rows at its working tolerance.
  The decision: a weight in plant's control (an explicit variant wins), an implicit step
  in odelia (an implicit variant wins and no explicit variant passes with fewer rows),
  or neither (nothing passes, or nothing saves rows).

KNOWN LIMITS, stated in advance
  Implicit variants get no invader gradients (plant cannot replay ARK programs).
  The frozen differences carry a noise near 5e-5 in E (TF24's leaf solve turns one ulp
  into 1e-9 of J), a third of d_I's eps/3; d_I's moves are read with that floor.

AMENDMENT 1, 03:30 UTC, before any frozen-difference run (stage 1 at its second run).
  The driver's THETA scales the parameter in strategy_default before add_strategies, and
  TF24's hyper-parameterisation then overwrites the derived parameters: d_I (from rho)
  goes back from 0.011 to 0.01 (check_theta.R), so THETA=d_I would give E = 0. a_dG1 and
  a_dG2 survive; lma's route is the total elasticity through k_l, r_l and nmass_l.
  Change: the frozen differences use THETA_AFTER=1 (theta_after.patch, driver copy
  harness_f/), which scales p$strategies[[1]]$pars[[THETA]] after add_strategies. Every
  frozen E is then the partial elasticity, the quantity plant's reverse mode reports and
  eps.csv was built from. Consequences:
  - R1's reference for every frozen quantity, lma included, is ld_1e-5.rds (plant, CK,
    1e-5), not the baseline's frozen (total) lma.
  - The baseline triple in the frozen instrument is replaced by plant replays of the
    baseline programs DEV/pi/runs/base_{2.85e-5,3.15e-5}.rds (plus the window test's
    ld_pin_base for 3e-5), which carry the partial elasticities of both roles. The pi
    frozen (total) lma triple is still reported as context.
  Stage 1's runs after the first come from harness_u/ (uptake_record.patch: records each
  accepted step's uptake per layer and each entry's; no number changes), for the optional
  chain-error diagnostic (chain_err.R).
