The implicit soil chain (ARK) in plant, three records: pre-registration
Written 2026-10-03 ~10:45 UTC, before any run of this test.

Setting
- DEV/lib_ark: odelia ark-step f11e753, phylloptim 378b083, plant ark-soil
  4dc59a40 (on state-weights 4555ea13, so WEIGHT_MAX works). Runs go from a
  fresh snapshot of plant-dev 1ffb6ab's harness (combined/snap3), with the soil
  clamp tallies of combined/run_record_clamps.patch applied to run_record.R.
- Plant's own adaptive runs at 3e-5, ATOL=1e-4, the 108 uniform introductions,
  the active knots as zero pulses, HMAX=15.
- Arm A: METHOD=ark, WEIGHT_SOIL=100 (arkc: the soil judged by the chain
  alone's estimate, at weight 100).
- Arm B: arm A plus rule A's WEIGHT (window/rule_A/weight_<record>.rds) and
  WEIGHT_MAX=100; the bound holds the soil at 100, so rule A acts on the members.
- Records: long drought, long-wet, episodic, each with both roles' gradients and
  the eight walks (lma and hmat at x0.5, x0.7, x1.4, x2); arm A also runs long
  drought's +-5% tolerance nudges (2.85e-5, 3.15e-5) with both roles' gradients
  and no walks.

Check, first and forward only: METHOD=ark WEIGHT_SOIL=100 on long drought at
3e-5, no cap, reproduces the driver's arkc bit for bit: 9786 accepted steps,
J 12.6425450956. If it does not, stop and report.

Criteria (as before)
- C1, never fails: no phase and no walk raises, and both roles' gradients are
  finite, on every record.
- C2, accuracy: per record and role, the distance from the same 1e-5 references
  (nudges/ld_1e-5.rds, combined/full/wet_1e-5.rds, phase1c/full/epi_1e-5.rds;
  median and largest over the 49 quantities, in eps) is no larger than plant's
  unweighted Cash-Karp run's, and the largest stays under eps/3.
- C3, reproducible (arm A, long drought): the +-5% nudges move every quantity of
  both roles by less than eps/3.

Reported, not pass/fail: costs (rows, forward, gradient run) against plant's
unweighted Cash-Karp runs (spot-check ld_3e-5, wet_base, epi_base) and against
the bounded combined Cash-Karp runs (combined/full/bnd_*); J's error against J*;
both roles' moves against the unweighted run; the soil's clamp tallies on each
sweep; anything that throws.

Stop rule: if a record refuses its gradient or a walk raises, that arm stops; the
refusal and its tallies are reported and no fix is tried. Each arm runs its
records cheapest first (episodic, long drought, long-wet).

Addendum, 11:35 UTC: the soil weight at which ARK meets the accuracy bar.
Written before any of these runs.
- Hypothesis: ARK's distance from 1e-5 comes from the soil's weight of 100, not
  from ARK itself (J's error is 100x Cash-Karp's, the error is broad, and
  long-wet, where the soil matters least, nearly passed).
- Test: arm B (METHOD=ark, rule A's WEIGHT, WEIGHT_MAX=100, HMAX=15, 3e-5,
  ATOL=1e-4, uniform 108, knots) at WEIGHT_SOIL = 10, 30 and 50, on long drought
  and episodic, both roles' gradients, no walks, no nudges; from plant-dev
  d4e0535's harness (snap4, clamp tallies built in), lib_ark. The weight-100
  point is arm B's existing runs (arkB_ld, arkB_epi). The hypothesis holds if
  the largest distance from 1e-5 falls with the weight, per record and role.
- Two accuracy readings per run and role, against the same 1e-5 references:
  (i) C2 as defined: median and largest no larger than unweighted Cash-Karp's,
      and the largest under eps/3;
  (ii) the budget reading: the largest distance under eps/3 and the median
      under 0.02 eps.
- Costs: the gradient run (forward + 6 rows)/7 against unweighted Cash-Karp
  (spot-check ld_3e-5, epi_base) and against bounded Cash-Karp (bnd_ld, bnd_epi).
- The pick: the largest weight that meets (ii) on both records and both roles;
  if none does, that is the finding. Then, for the pick only, long drought's
  +-5% tolerance nudges with both roles' gradients (C3).
- Stop rule: a weight whose run refuses a gradient or raises stops there (its
  other record is not run); its refusal and tallies are reported.
