P0. The patch is inert when unset (check, before anything else).
  Episodic under rule A with HMAX unset reproduces window/drv/episodic_rule.rds
  bit for bit: identical st (every column), J, attempts and member evaluations.
  If not, stop and fix the patch.

P1. Never fails (R5). On each record's rule-A + HMAX=15 program, plant's replay
  of the stand, its gradient, the identical invader's walk and gradient, and
  every invader walk (lma and hmat at x0.5 and x2; x0.7 and x1.4 where run)
  finish with a finite J'. Any raise fails P1.

P2. Cost (R7). Let S = 1 - X/X_unweighted, X = rows (sum over accepted steps of
  the members carried) and X = forward member evaluations, from the driver's
  OUT. The cap stays if it gives up less than a fifth of rule A's saving:
  (S_A - S_cap) / S_A < 0.2, for rows AND forward, on each record.
  Rule A's measured savings (rows / forward): long drought 22.7% / 22.8%,
  long-wet 21.2% / 20.5%, episodic 21.0% / 20.8%. So the capped run must save
  more than about 18.1% / 18.3%, 17.0% / 16.4%, 16.8% / 16.7%.
  Prediction, from splitting rule A's long steps evenly with no controller
  feedback: +0.16%, +0.16% and +1.5% rows over rule A at 15 days, i.e. about 1%,
  1% and 6% of the saving given up; under 0.2% at 26 days.

P3. Accuracy (R1). On each HMAX=15 program, every quantity of both roles
  (ln J and every elasticity with an eps in docs/measurements/eps.csv, the
  window phase's 98) moves at most 0.08 eps against the unweighted run, as rule
  A did (0.016 eps weight alone, 0.078 eps with thinning). A largest move in
  (0.08, 0.10] eps is reported as marginal ("about 0.08"); over 0.10 eps fails.
  Secondary, reported but not pass/fail: the range-end invaders' ln J' moves
  against the unweighted runs' (expected under 0.08 eps).

P4. Mechanism (HMAX=26, no pass criterion). For y' = -y/tau at tau_s = 7 d,
  Cash-Karp's fourth stage goes negative past 15 d and the step is unstable
  past 26 d. Decision rule: if every walk on the HMAX=26 programs runs,
  stability is what the walk needs and positivity is not; if a walk raises
  under 26 but not under 15, positivity is needed. Weak expectation: 26 runs,
  since the unweighted episodic program already takes 63 steps over 15 d (to
  22 d) and lma x2 runs on it; but rule B's raise followed a 33.8-d step and
  sits on a 22-d one, so this is uncertain.

P5. Verdict rule.
  - P1, P2 and P3 pass at HMAX=15: the cap stays, and invader sub-steps are not
    built (deleted for the cap, as the design's deletion list allows).
  - P1 fails at 15 days: the cap does not protect invaders; sub-steps (or a
    pool update that stays non-negative) are needed.
  - P2 fails on any record: report the cap with the thinned schedule too, and
    sub-steps are to be built if the cap costs a fifth or more.
  - P3 fails: the cap's program moves a quantity; report which, and rule A's
    own move on the same record where it exists.
