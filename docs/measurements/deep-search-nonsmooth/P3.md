# P3: branches as data (move 2, the record → replay shape)

Proposer 3. Assigned move: move a decision offline. Every candidate below is a
different answer to one question: which of the forward's decisions does each later
pass hold, and which does it make again? Code read: odelia `origin/ODELIA-55`
(`ode_interface.hpp`, `ode_step.hpp`, `ode_solver_internal.hpp`, `implicit_node.hpp`),
plant `PLANT-110` at the brief's path (`tf24_strategy.h` `solve_leaf`,
`leaf_solved_points`, the TF24 sweep's `collar_at` call; `patch.h` split;
`scm.h` `run_mutant`), phylloptim `origin/PHYLLOPTIM-19` (`maximise_profit_over_collar`,
`replay_operating_point`, `collar_at`), and the JT scratch diff
(`JT_out/stop_plant.diff`). Every number is marked **(m)** measured, with its source,
or **(e)** estimated, with its derivation.

## Triage: 3

It decides what a recording means to every pass that reads one (forward, sweep,
walks, replays on a grid), across three packages, and the requirements arrive partly
as mechanisms ("declare, don't handle", "one account").

## Requirements ledger

- **R1 Reproducible.** Nudge rms ≤ 0.006 (max ≤ 0.1) on dry, sharp gate, 3e-4 ± 5%;
  today 0.048 (m, JT part 2).
- **R2 Continuous on one grid.** Gradients from passes at θ′ over the θ₀ grid: no jump
  above 0.01ε across the analysis steps (1e-2 chords, 1e-3 Jacobian, 1e-4 central
  differences, ±10% resident).
  - Unmeasured.
  - *Challenged upward:* is the bar per jump, or on the total roughness over one
    analysis step? Is any curvature taken by differencing gradients? Candidate C
    hangs on the answer.
- **R3 Cost.** At most +14% rows over the unresolved run at the same tolerance.
  - Rows are the cost: 1.11e-4 s per row forward plus 2.4e-3 s per step, and 3.56e-4 s
    per row in the sweep (ledger).
  - Evaluations are reported beside rows.
- **R4 Declare, don't handle.** About one member per new non-smooth point.
  - *Challenged upward:* detecting, locating and ending the row at a point can need one
    member. Holding a point's **branch** in later passes needs the point's own code to
    read its branch from the record.
  - That reading is free for an inner solve that already records what it solved for
    (TF24's leaf: #10–#15). It is not free for an expression clamp (#5, #7, #16, #19–#24).
  - Of those clamps, only #16 is met in accepted steps in any number, and it changes
    slope by about 0.5% (inventory).
  - Confirm that R4 means detect, locate and stop with one member, and that branch
    holding is wanted only for inner solves.
- **R5 Events and one account of time.**
  - The R interface and the forward's behaviour stay unchanged.
  - Every division of time (scheduled times, controller step ends, rain knots, located
    points) is described by one rule.
- **R6 Exact for its own map.** Central differences match the sweep within 0.01ε.
  - Today 0.0027ε on a pinned, split run (m, JT P3-S3).
- **R7 Walks.** An invader (×0.5 to ×2) walks the resident's recorded steps and field.
  It has zero density and does not feed back on the soil.
- **R8 Not worse.**
  - The per-node split (16 502 node splits on dry) keeps its cost and accuracy.
  - The rain knots, in the soil's substeps and in the insertions, keep theirs.
- **R9 Jumps (J kinds).** Their incidence on current builds is unknown.
  - *Asked:* the operating-point census (Determined, HydraulicShutdown) and the
    layer-rate clamp count in **accepted** rows on each bank record.
  - *Challenged:* #13's jump comes from the bracket's 1e-3 MPa collapse tolerance. It is
    an artifact of the solver, and could be made continuous where it arises rather than
    handled downstream.
- **R10** The averaged gate is out of scope.

**Scarce resource.** Row ends.
- R3 leaves room for about 0.14 × 4786 ≈ 670 extra row ends on dry.
- There are 14 728 branch changes of the leaf alone (m).
- So at most one change in 22 can end a row. Every other change must be carried as
  data the existing rows already hold, or as an error.

## The floor

Change nothing in any later pass. Have the forward end a row at the first and last
located zero of each cross-block margin that changes sign inside it.
- For TF24 the margin is f_hi, the leaf's marginal profit at the dry end. phylloptim
  already computes it on every solve, and it is smooth through the switch.
- The row ends are then ordinary times, held by replays, walks and the sweep, as every
  row end is today.
- This is the JT "stop" build (part 2), declared through one System member instead of
  plant scratch code.

| R3 | rows +12.8% (454 494 → 512 679), steps +11.5% (4786 → 5334), evaluations ≈ +20% | (m) |
| R6 | 0.0027ε on the soil-split build | (m); no reason it worsens with stops (e) |
| R7 | walks take the new rows as rows: +12.8% walk rows. #10's reach is soil only, and a walk reads the soil from the record, so the invader sees #10 only as C1 in its own net production (envelope theorem; inventory note 10) | (e) |
| R8 | split untouched | by construction |
| R4 | one member, where the stop locates through the split's per-node evaluation | (e) |
| R2 | estimated **pass**, derived below | (e) |
| R9 | unknown; fails exactness wherever a J occurs in an accepted row (see "Each pass") | — |

**The R2 estimate.** A pass at θ′ that decides branches again flips a leaf's arm at a
stage whenever its switch time crosses that stage. Each flip moves the gradient by
about (θ/J/ε)·λ·h·ΔG_i·b_j: one leaf's share of a cluster, times one stage weight.

- **The cluster's coefficient.** From part 4 (m): one run's Σ|c| ≈ 0.17ε, 90% of it in
  30 steps, at a median |φ| of 0.02–0.06. So (θ/J/ε)λhΔG ≈ 0.005ε / 0.04 ≈ 0.125ε per
  top cluster at h ≈ 2 d.
- **The size of one flip.** Divide by 51 leaves and multiply by a mean b_j ≈ 0.25. That
  gives about 6e-4ε in a 2-d row and about 1.4e-4ε in a stop's 0.44-d row (e).
- **The number of flips.** Let τ = dt*/d ln θ, in days. Over a step Δ in ln θ, about
  51 leaves × 30 clusters × τΔ / (0.17 × 0.44 d) ≈ 2e4·τΔ flips occur. Their signs are
  mixed.
- **The roughness of a 1e-2 chord** is then √(200τ) × 1.4e-4 ≈ 0.002ε·√τ. It stays
  under 0.01ε unless τ is above about 25 d (e).
- Every single flip is under 0.01ε (e).

The floor therefore meets every ledger line it can be judged on. It fails none by
number. R2 is estimated, not measured; spike U1 below settles it.

## Candidates

All three use move 2. They differ in what the program's data is.

**A [first thought]: the branches are held in every pass, and the row ends are
unchanged.**
- *Commitment:* each evaluation's branch is decided once, by the forward, and read by
  every later pass.
- *Pays for:* R2. A replay at θ′ evaluates a map that is smooth in θ′.
- *Costs:* a placement entry in phylloptim; walks over the recording.
- *Bad at:* R1 is untouched. The forward and the sweep are today's, at 0.048 (m).
- *Wins when:* the forward's jitter is already resolved by something else, and only
  passes at θ′ need smoothness.

**B: every branch change ends a row.** Each row holds one branch per leaf. Ends at
zeros are located again by replays and moved by the sweep's implicit function.
- *Commitment:* a row never holds a branch change.
- *Pays for:*
  - R1: ≤ 0.002 (e); bracketing already gives this (m).
  - R2: smooth.
  - R9 exactly: moving the end between two rows of held branches produces the term
    (f_A − f_B)·dt*/dθ with no further code.
- *Costs:* about 14 728 extra row ends, so steps +308% and rows ≈ +300% (e), against
  +14%.
- *Wins when:* changes are isolated, under about 670 a run (J kinds, rarely met clamps).
  This is also what the per-node split already is, for one block.

**C: the floor's stop, plus held branches and located stop ends in every pass over
the grid.**
- *Commitment:* a recording is the program. Every pass over it holds the branches its
  evaluations took and the times it stepped to. Only zeros (split cuts and stop ends)
  are located again.
- *Pays for:*
  - R1: 0.002 (m).
  - R2: smooth by construction.
  - R6: the replay's map is its sweep's map.
  - R9: through the moved stop ends.
- *Costs:*
  - About 160 lines and 4 names beyond the floor (e).
  - About +7% evaluations per replay, for the retakes at relocated stop ends (e).
  - Bias at θ′ from the cluster's change of shape.
- *Wins when:* the local analyses take differences of gradients finer than the flips,
  that is, when U1 fails the floor.

**Winner: the floor.**
- **A** fails R1: 0.048 against 0.006 (m).
  - A's held branches are also *less accurate* at θ′ than branches decided again. A
    switch held at θ₀'s stage while θ′ moves it by τΔ costs about K·τΔ.
  - K = Σ (θ/J/ε)λΔG over the cluster steps ≈ 0.6–1.9ε per day (e, from the
    coefficients above, with or without sign cancellation).
  - So at ±10% and τ = 1 d the bias is 0.06–0.19ε (e), against 0.002ε of jitter.
- **B** fails R3: +300% rows (e) against +14%.
- **C** pays only for R2, on which the floor is estimated to pass. Its relocation is
  needed to stop A's bias, which costs replay evaluations and a field. It is staged
  behind U1 (see "The design"). It is not built now.

## The commitment

A branch change ends a piece of the blocks that read it: a node's own piece where only
that node reads it, and the row where every node does.

**Kept true by:** two lists the System fills, one per reach.
- `sign_values`: margins whose change only their own block reads. It exists, and it
  drives the per-node split.
- `stop_values`: margins whose change crosses blocks. It is new, and it drives the
  stop.

A point listed in neither is not handled, and that is visible. A point in the wrong
list is the measured failure: splitting the node at f_hi gives 0.044, against 0.002
for the stop (m, JT P3-S1). A new point therefore costs its margin in the right list
and nothing else.

## Kill question

**Assumption whose falsity makes this unnecessary:** the leaf switch moves the
gradient through the soil, whose rates every node reads, so no piece of one block can
carry it.

**Verdict: survives.** From the ledger facts:
- Splitting the switching node changes nothing (0.048 → 0.044).
- Splitting the soil alone at the cluster's ends leaves 0.010, the nodes reading the
  main step's soil (m, part 3).
- Only ending the row reaches 0.002 (m).

## What survives deletion

- **`stop_values`** (a System member, with a concept beside `SplitsSignChanges`).
  Without it R1 fails (0.048) and R4 fails, since each new point would need its own
  stop code.
- **The stop in `SolverInternal::step`.** On an accepted step, before its split, it
  retakes the step to the first located zero, then makes the next step end at the
  last. R1, and R3 at +12.8%.
- **`arm_margin_dry`** (phylloptim keeps f_hi; plant exposes it as an aux). R1: it is
  the one margin with a measured effect. The wet margin and the arm aux of the JT build
  are deleted: no ledger line holds them.
- **The per-node evaluation at a fraction of the step (`node_value_at`)**, taking an aux
  index rather than being copied (`node_value_at_aux` in JT). It locates the zero
  cheaply: 51 node-sized evaluations rather than full ones, about +3% evaluations (e).
  Without it the stop locates with full evaluations at about +8–16% (e). R3 is about
  rows, so this is held by the cost of evaluations, not by a ledger bar.
- No other name. The step threshold (0.5 d) is deleted: it gives 0.0081 > 0.006 (m,
  JT S3).

## What this settles

- No plant code decides where a row stops. Plant lists margins and odelia stops.
- No replay, walk or sweep changes. Stopped rows are ordinary rows. The sweep reads
  each evaluation's branch as today.
- No node is split at f_hi: measured useless.
- No soil split at switches: 0.010 at best, measured.
- No per-change rows: B's +300%.
- No ×0.1 tolerance near closing edges (ETT): the stop gives 0.002 at +12.8% rows,
  against ETT's 0.025 at +8.6% (m).
- Invaders need nothing for #10 (R7). The soil they read is recorded, and their own
  net production is C1 through their own switch.

## What this makes hard

- **Differences of gradients on one grid finer than the flips.** If U1 measures
  roughness above 0.01ε over a 1e-2 chord or a 1e-3 Jacobian column, the floor fails
  R2. Cope by building C (specified below).
- **J kinds.**
  - A J declared in `stop_values` gets a row end at its zero. That end is held by the
    sweep, so the term (f_A − f_B)·dt*/dθ is missing. That is a first-order error that
    does not shrink with h.
  - The end evaluation, which is also the next row's first stage, takes whichever arm
    the zero's rounding gives it. For a K kind that is a single-leaf term of about
    1e-4–2.5e-4ε per cut (e). It is part of the measured 0.002. For a J kind it is a
    rate error the size of the jump.
  - Cope with C's moved stop ends (below), triggered by the R9 census.
- **Points that are not inner solves (clamps).** They are detected and stopped like
  any other, but every pass decides their branches again.

## Kill condition

R2 measured (U1) fails the floor, or R9's census finds J changes in accepted rows. The
design then hands off to **C**. C adds to the floor and does not replace it, so
nothing built now is undone. B takes over only if rows stop being scarce, that is, if
R3's +14% rises above about +300%.

## The design

### Parts

1. **odelia: a stop list and the stop.**
   - A System may declare `stop_values(std::vector<double>&)`, one margin per block,
     read beside `sign_values` after each evaluation (`Step::take_step` and
     `step()` already read `sign_values`).
   - It also declares `stop_fraction(step, bool last) -> double`. That is the plant
     code that runs the existing `taken_step::sign_changes` rule on each stop block
     and returns the earliest or latest zero in (1e-6, 1 − 1e-6).
   - In `SolverInternal::step`, between acceptance and `split()`: if the step holds a
     zero, set the step to `u × h` and take it again. The step sizes come from a
     retake, not from dense output, because a truncated dense output would read the
     stages past the zero, which is the whole cluster.
   - Then mark the next step to end at the last zero of its own cluster.
   - About 40 lines (e). `step_to` and `step_by` are untouched: pinned passes never
     stop.
2. **phylloptim: keep f_hi.** `arm_margin_dry_`, NaN where no collar solve ran,
   reset per solve. About 8 lines (e).
3. **plant: list it.**
   - TF24 writes the margin to an aux.
   - `Patch::stop_values` lists each node's aux.
   - `Patch::stop_fraction` reuses `node_value_at`, given an aux index.
   - About 60 lines (e), from JT's 149 added lines less logging, the scan, and the wet
     and kind aux.

### What the forward decides and records

- **Each evaluation's branches.** TF24 already records a collar and an arm per leaf
  solve (`leaf_solved_point`). Unchanged.
- **Where each row ends:**
  - the controller's choice;
  - a scheduled time (an insertion or event);
  - a rain knot;
  - the first or last zero of a stop margin.
  All are recorded as `instruction{time, step_size}`. Unchanged.
- **Where each node's piece ends**, at the zeros of its net production (`split_blocks`).
  Unchanged.

### Each pass

- **Forward at double.**
  - Decides every branch from its own stage state, as today.
  - Ends a row at a stop margin's first and last zero.
  - A stage that lands on a branch change takes the side the margin's sign gives
    (ties go to the bound in `maximise_profit_over_collar`). For a K kind both sides
    give the same rate to the zero's rounding.
- **Sweep (θ₀).**
  - Reads rows, states, and each evaluation's collar and arm (`solved_scope` with
    const values → `replay_operating_point`). Unchanged.
  - Holds stopped row ends as times. For a K kind the derivative of the map in that
    end's position is f_before − f_after = 0, so holding it is first-order exact apart
    from the end-evaluation term above.
  - Split cuts move by `implicit_value`, as today.
- **A resident pinned to the grid at θ′.**
  - Holds the times. Decides every branch again from its own stages, as today, and
    splits nodes again from its own state.
  - Its map is continuous in θ′. Its gradient has flips of about 1e-4–6e-4ε (e).
- **Invader walk.** Holds the rows and the recorded field. Decides its own leaves'
  branches. Holds the run's split cuts for the copies of the run's nodes. All
  unchanged.
- **Pinned replay from times alone** (the R `ode_times` route). It is a forward on held
  times, which decides everything. Unchanged.

### The one account of time (R5)

Time is divided in two ways:

- **At a time.** Every pass holds it: a scheduled event (node introduction, harvest,
  pulse, climate extreme), a rain knot, a controller step end, a stop end.
- **At a zero of one block's margin inside a row.** Every pass locates it again, and
  the sweep moves it: a per-node split cut. Inside a piece the branches are the
  record's.

Events keep acting between rows, at times. Their R interface does not move.

### Staged: C, built only on its trigger

The trigger is U1 failing R2, or R9's census finding J changes in accepted rows.
Everything here adds to the floor.

1. **Branches held in passes over the grid.**
   - `leaf_solved_points::store_into(into)` keeps the arms `into` arrives with before
     clearing it. A walk already hands each evaluation a pre-filled copy
     (`stepper_step`: `solved_scratch_ = *recorded`). `solve_leaf` then places on a
     held arm where one is present, and searches only where none is.
   - phylloptim adds `Leaf::place_on_arm(kind, collar)`:
     - an Interior arm takes the root of dprofit from `collar`, past the dry bound if
       the root lies there, up to the stem table's end at P99;
     - a bound arm takes its bound;
     - it reports whether the leaf's own margin agrees with the held arm.
   - That agreement, tallied, is the grid's radius (OBJECTIVES: Diagnosed).
   - A resident pass over the kept recording builds its own field and splits its nodes
     again.
   - `run_mutant` clears the residents' arms with the field, since an invader's leaves
     are not the run's.
   - About 100 lines (e). Needs spike S1.
2. **Stop ends as zeros.**
   - `instruction` gains `zero_of`: which stop margin ended the row.
   - A replay at θ′ steps to the recorded end, locates that margin's zero on the dense
     output, and takes the row again to it.
   - The sweep moves the end by `implicit_value` on the margin at the end state. That
     yields (f_A − f_B)·dt*/dθ for a J and about 0 for a K.
   - The end's evaluation takes the far side, by placing the end 1e-9 of the step past
     the zero.
   - This removes A's bias from the cluster's shift and keeps the part from its change
     of shape. About 60 lines (e). It costs about 530 retakes × 5 evaluations ≈ +7% of
     a replay (e).

### Spikes that settle the unknowns

- **U1 (R2).** Run 21 pinned gradient passes on the stopped grid at θ₀(1 + k·1e-3),
  k = −10…10, along TF24_cost_scale, plus 11 at 1e-4 spacing. Fit a quadratic. The
  residual rms is the roughness. About 1.5 h (e: 21 × 250 s). The floor fails R2 if it
  exceeds 0.01ε.
- **U2 (τ).** Take a per-leaf census of f_hi switch times from two pinned forwards at
  θ₀(1 ± 1e-2), split into the cluster's shift and its change of shape. About 2 min.
  This gives the bias of C without part 2.
  - The P4_traj runs at ±1e-4 (JT part 4) may already hold it, offline.
- **S1 (C only).** Check that dprofit and uptake are finite past the dry bound, up to
  the largest collar move U2 implies, on dry's switch steps. If not, C's smoothness
  holds only on one side of each switch.
- **R9 census.** Count Determined, HydraulicShutdown and layer-rate clamps in accepted
  rows on each bank record.
