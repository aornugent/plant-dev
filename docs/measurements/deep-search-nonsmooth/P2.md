# P2 — move the system boundary: where does the leaf's switch stand?

Framing move 3: solve the problem where it stands — in the model's representation, in
phylloptim, in plant's caller, or by reframing a requirement — rather than in the stepper.
The search below tries each of those places in turn. Three of them fail on numbers. One
measured treatment survives: the forward stops at the cluster's first and last switch. What
the move changes is how little the stepper has to own around that stop. The forward locates
the times once. From then on they are ordinary step ends. Replays, walks and the sweep are
untouched, and the model declares one value.

Numbers are marked **(m)** measured, **(e)** estimated, or **(s)** measured here by the
spike in `deep/spike/`. That is libJTstop in detection-only mode (`JT_STOP=0`) at JT's
settings on dry: inset schedule, 149 nodes, tolerance 3e-4. The reference is A's own
whole-run roots (`S1b_census.err`, 14 728 roots).

## Triage: 3 — the representation spans three repos and the program every pass reads; the requirements arrive partly as mechanisms ("stop", "split"); a spike settled two questions

## The domain: what is switching, and is it the population or the discretisation?

**Why the stand switches as one.** Every node reads one soil and has one species' psi_crit
and cost curve. The switch Interior → BoundaryCrit is the zero of f_hi, the marginal profit
at the dry bound. That zero is set mainly by the shared soil potential against psi_crit. A
node's size, light and rooting move it little:
- In the 9.70 cluster, every node alive switches: nodes 0 to 52, born 0 to 9.70 yr, from
  seedlings to the tallest trees. 90% of their roots fall within 0.9 d (s), and 48 of 56
  switches fall within 0.44 d (m).
- A 1% change in TF24_cost_scale or lma moves the whole cluster by a median 0.25–0.29 d (s).
- So the stand's whole spread of ages and sizes shifts the switch about as much as a 1.5%
  change in one trait (e).

The window's width is set by how fast the soil's potential runs through the stand's narrow
range of thresholds at the end of a drydown. It is a property of the population and the
soil.

**What a node stands for.** On the birth-date coordinate a node is a sample of the density
at birth date b. The soil's uptake is a quadrature over b of each cohort's transpiration.
In the continuum, the switch time t_s(b) is a smooth function. The soil's sensitivity
integrand G(t) = ∫ n(b) ∂E/∂θ(b, t) db is then a ramp across the window
[min t_s, max t_s]. Its slope jumps only at the window's two edges, where the first and last
cohort switch. The 50 jumps inside are how 50 nodes sample that ramp. The edges are real,
and so is the ramp's narrowness: 0.44 d against steps of about 2 d.

**So smoothing is not the answer, and the averaged gate shows why.** A Cash–Karp step
integrates a transition of width W with an error that, relative to a jump of the same size,
falls as follows (`spike/ramp.R`; steps of random length, rms over placements) (s):

| W, in steps | 0.1 | 0.22 | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| linear ramp | 0.74 | 0.50 | 0.19 | 0.09 | 0.046 | 0.018 |
| smooth (C1) ramp | 0.81 | 0.61 | 0.26 | 0.074 | 0.017 | 0.003 |

- **Today's cluster.** It is W ≈ 0.22 step. Representing it exactly as a ramp, by a
  kink-aware quadrature in b or by spreading the members' switches, would cut its error
  by about half (e). R1 needs a cut by 8.
- **What R1 would need.** W ≳ 0.7 step, about 1.5 d on dry. Even that holds only while the
  controller keeps its steps there. A step's length is the controller's choice, up to the
  15-day cap, so no fixed width in the model stays wide against the steps.
- **The averaged gate.** Its knee is 2–3 d wide in 1.5–5 d steps, so W ≈ 0.5–1.5, a ratio of
  0.03–0.26. It still jittered at 0.110 because its jump is large: it enters every
  newborn's density.

Smoothing scales the error by W/h, and the controller, not the model, sets h.

**Who carries the error.** Only the resident's soil row (m: the node split at f_hi changed
nothing, 0.048 → 0.044). Net production is the optimised profit, which stays C1 through the
switch. So:
- an invader's own rates are C1 at its own switches;
- an invader walking a recorded field never meets the problem (R7).

## The floor

Do nothing to the numerics; report the nudge as the tolerance knob's error.
— fails R1: 0.048 rms (max 0.176) vs 0.006 (m). It passes the challenged bar of ε/3. If the
user confirms ε/3, the floor is the design.

## Candidates

A [first thought] **Move 3, into the model's representation.** The stand's switch is
represented as the ramp it is: a birth-date quadrature that splits each interval at the
cohort currently switching, or a leaf whose bound is soft (a barrier cost, as phylloptim's
product objectives already have).
- Commitment: the rates' sensitivity is continuous in time, so the integrator never sees a
  jump.
- Pays for: nothing on the ledger. At W = 0.22 step the error falls to 0.5–0.6 of a jump (s).
  A soft bound wide enough to matter (≳ 1.5 d) changes the leaf's physics near psi_crit, and
  with it ln J and every reference.
- Costs: a new representation in plant's uptake sum, or a new cost curve in phylloptim. The
  science owner objects to the second.
- Wins when: the transition is naturally several steps wide. That would be a leaf whose
  stomata close over a week of drying, as SOX-like objectives do, where nothing needs to be
  located at all.

B **Move 3, into plant's caller.** The pilot that already sets the tolerance window
(`control_window`, 54 uniform nodes at 1e-3) also reports each cluster's first and last
switch. The run takes those as given times, like the rain knots' entries. odelia is not
changed. There are no retakes, and replays hold the times like any other.
- Commitment: every division of time a run needs is known before it starts.
- Pays for: R1 only if the pilot's edges sit on the run's. They do not. The pilot's first
  edges are off by a median of 0.21 d (90%: 1.4 d), and its brackets ±0.1 d miss 11% of
  the run's switches (±0.25 d: 5.4%) (s). With a margin wide enough to hold the cluster,
  the bracket is about as long as the step it replaces. R1 is estimated at 0.005–0.01 and
  unmeasured.
- Costs: rows about +10% (e) and evaluations about +10% (e), with no retakes. Lines: about
  60 in plant and R (e).
- Wins when: the pass that supplies the times runs at the differentiated run's own node
  schedule and tolerance, for example a workflow that always replays before it sweeps. The
  times are then exact (E6: 36× with shared boundaries (m)) and need no retake.

C **Move 3, onto the requirement's boundary.** R1 and R2 ask different passes for different
things:
- R1 is about the adaptive forward, the only pass where the controller makes incidental
  choices. It needs the clusters bracketed there.
- R2 is about pinned passes, which make no choices. It needs only continuity, which held
  rows already give.

So a time located from the state is located once, by the forward, at the first and last
root of a declared value in an accepted step. The step is cut and retaken there, and from
then on the time is an ordinary step end.
- Commitment: see below.
- Pays for: R1 at 0.0020 rms (max 0.007) (m, JT S2: the stop is exactly this, with "pinned
  replays and the sweep untouched"). R3 at +12.8% rows and +11.5% steps (m). R4–R8 by
  construction (below).
- Costs: about 100 lines (e) and three names. The forward's evaluations rise about 20% (e)
  because of the retakes. Resolution fades for passes more than about 0.5% from θ₀ (s).
- Wins when: the gradients that must be reproducible are those at the grid's own θ₀, and
  passes at θ′ need only continuity. That is the ledger as written.

D **Move 3, into the soil.** The soil owns its time. Each step holding switches integrates
the soil again in pieces meeting at the first and last f_hi root. The nodes are untouched.
- Commitment: the jump is the soil's, so the soil's integration resolves it.
- Pays for: R1 only partly: 0.010 (m) vs 0.006. The residual is the nodes reading the main
  step's soil, plus 68 of the 234 switch steps already substepped, which are skipped (m).
- Costs: +8.5% evaluations (m), rows unchanged (m). A soil block in the sweep's pieces.
- Wins when: rows are scarcer than the bar, and 0.01 is enough.

E **Move 3 refused: pieces inside held rows, located in every pass.** Each pass integrates
a row that holds a cluster as three pieces meeting at its first and last root, found again
at θ′. The sweep moves the piece ends through odelia's implicit node.
- Commitment: resolution at any θ′ on one grid.
- Pays for: R1 (e, as C), and resolution at the resident's ±10% (e).
- Costs: about +11% evaluations in every pass, forward and replays (e). Whole-state pieces
  in the sweep. An implicit term at each piece end. About 300 lines (e).
- Wins when: R2 becomes "resolved at θ′", or J kinds turn out frequent (R9).

(F, **delete R6**: a continuous adjoint with its own error control on the gradient's
quadrature would refine at every switch with no declaration at all. It fails R6 by
construction. It fails R2 because its backward steps move with θ′. It fails R3 because it
refines each of the 50 jumps (e: +50–250% backward steps). It wins only if no one needs the
discrete map's own derivative, and the record → replay design says someone does.)

**Winner: C.**
- *Eliminations:*
  - The floor fails R1 (0.048 vs 0.006).
  - A fails R1: a ramp of 0.22 step leaves 0.5–0.6 of the error (s), and the width that

  - B fails R1 on its own spike: 11% of switches lie outside the pilot's brackets, and its

## The commitment

A time located from the state is located once, by the adaptive forward that chooses the
steps. From then on it is a step end like the controller's.

Kept true by:
- The program's row (`instruction`: a time and a size) carries no record of why a row
  ended. A replay has nothing it could locate again.
- The locating code lives only in the accept path of the adaptive step loop.
  `advance_recorded`, which replays and the sweep use, never reaches it.

## Kill question

Assumption whose falsity makes this unnecessary: R1's bar of 0.006 is needed, so a looser
tolerance run must be able to estimate the gradient's error.

Verdict: survives, with the challenge logged. OBJECTIVES' Predictable asks each knob's
error to fall at its order so a looser run estimates it. The tolerance's own move
(0.018) is a third of today's jitter (0.048), so without the stop the tolerance knob

together, which needs that reading. If the user says the tolerance knob's estimate is not
consumed, R1 drops to ε/3, and the floor (0.176 < 0.33) wins.

## What this settles

- No relocation of step ends in replays, and no implicit term at step ends.
- No new kind of row, no new record field, and no change to `advance_recorded`, walks or
  the sweep.
- No soil split, no node split at f_hi, and no pilot output beyond today's window.
- No model code beyond one member per non-smooth point. A new K point is declared and the
  forward stops at it.
- Invaders need nothing: their own switches are C1 in their rates.
- One account of time divisions (R5): every division of the System's time is a row end in
  the program. In the forward it comes from one of three sources: a schedule entry
  (introductions, actions, the rain knots' entries), the controller, or a declared value's
  first or last root in an accepted step. Every later pass takes the rows as recorded.
- A split cut is not a division of the System's time. It integrates one node's block again
  inside a row, and it is located again in every pass because its node's whole jump sits at
  the cut. A step end at a cluster's edge carries about 1/N of the cluster's jump.

## What this makes hard

- **Resolution away from θ₀.** Beyond about ±0.5% of a trait the stops no longer bracket
  their clusters (s). There, and across the resident's ±10%, the gradient carries today's
  unresolved error: about 0.05ε, continuous but first order in the step. If forced, rebuild
  the grid for each resident step of a few percent (OBJECTIVES: "several grids or a finer
  one serve it"), or hand off to E.
- **The cost of the retakes.** About +20% forward evaluations (e) for +12.8% rows (m), and
  most cuts buy nothing (m). If R3 tightens, skip cuts where the window's factor has
  loosened the tolerance. That removes the 148 cuts after b = 25 (m), about −28% of cuts
  (e). Not built.
- **J kinds (R9).** A held time drops the term from the jump's location moving with θ. A
  declared J point would be stopped at in the forward, but its derivative would still lack
  that term.
- **Coarse node schedules.** The one-grid continuity bound grows as 1/N nodes per cluster.

## Kill condition

- R2 is upgraded to require resolved gradients over the resident's ±10% on one grid, or
  curvatures by chords at 1e-2 fail ε/3 (most at risk: a_dG2, whose curvature ε is 0.081
  against an elasticity ε of 0.019 (m)). Either hands off to **E**.
- J kinds turn out frequent (R9). That hands off to E's implicit term.
- The user confirms R1 at ε/3. Then **the floor** wins.
- A workflow that always replays before it sweeps makes **B** with exact times (taken from
  the forward's own detection) cheaper than the retakes.

## The design

**The declared value (phylloptim, plant).** The leaf's collar solve already evaluates f_hi,
the marginal profit at the dry end of the feasible collar interval, on every solve
(Interior ⇔ f_lo > 0 > f_hi; BoundaryCrit ⇔ f_hi ≥ 0). phylloptim keeps it as a member,
reset per solve. TF24_Strategy names it, through its aux, as its step-ending value. This is
the model's whole share. It also describes the domain: an optimum with a bound reports how
far it is from that bound becoming active. f_lo (BoundarySoil, #11) would be a second value
of the same member. It is not declared, because dry and long drought never reach it (m).

**Locating (plant Patch, using odelia's rule).** After each evaluation, `Patch::sign_values`
appends the declared value for every node after the net productions the split reads, as
blocks n..2n−1. Those values already come from the stages' leaf solves, so this costs
nothing. `Patch::stop_fraction(step, last)` runs `taken_step::sign_changes` on each node's
block. That is the ends and four inner stages, the pair rule, and regula falsi on the
step's dense output with the sampled field, all of which the split already uses. It returns
the first root in (1e-6, 1 − 1e-6), or the last when asked (m: 56/56 switches found in the
9.70 steps, within 0.0012 of the step).

**Cutting (odelia).** In the accept path, after the error test keeps a step and before its
split:
1. If the System supplies `stop_fraction`, a concept member checked with `if constexpr`,
   ask for the first root. If the previous row ended at a first root, ask for the last.
2. If a root lies inside, retake the step with h × u. The retaken step is the row, and the
   split then runs on it as usual.

There is one retake per step. The state is one bool local to the advance loop.

**Recording.** Nothing new. The row is `instruction{time, step_size}`, as for any step.

## Each pass

- **Forward at double (adaptive, recording).** Each accepted step reads the declared values
  at its stages, which come free with the leaf solves. Where a node's value changes sign,
  the step is cut at the cluster's first root and retaken, and the next step at its last.
  Both cuts are recorded as ordinary rows. On dry: 530 cuts, +11.5% steps, +12.8% rows (m).
  Rain knots, events, introductions and the per-node split are unchanged (R5, R8).
- **Pinned replay of the resident at θ′.** Unchanged. It takes every row as recorded,
  searches its leaves again, and places nothing. Near θ₀ (≤ 0.1% of a trait) the stop rows
  still bracket every switch (s). At 1%, 8–29% of switches fall into neighbouring rows (s).
  At 10%, most do, and the gradient there carries today's unresolved, continuous error.
- **Invader's walk.** Unchanged. It walks the resident's rows, the stop rows included, and
  its recorded field. Its own arm switches are C1 in its rates, so it needs no stop (R7).
- **Reverse sweep.** Unchanged. It transposes rows, the stop rows included, and loads each
  evaluation's recorded arms. The rows are fixed constants of the map, so R6 holds as for
  any pinned program. No implicit term is added.
